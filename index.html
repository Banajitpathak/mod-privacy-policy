Privacy Policy

App: My Office Diary (MOD) • Developer: Banajit Pathak • Effective Date: October 2026

Executive Summary: My Office Diary is an offline-first productivity tool. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, diary records, or location details. Everything stays securely on your device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

My Office Diary is designed with complete user privacy as a foundational principle:

  • Local Storage Only: All entries—including activities, tours, vehicle logbooks, action points, and expense receipts—are stored exclusively in a private SQLite database residing on your device.
  • No Cloud Servers: The app does not operate external user accounts or background tracking servers. No data is automatically synced to the cloud.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: The app contains no third-party tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, or advertising identifiers.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

My Office Diary requests strictly essential Android permissions to deliver core functionality:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to alert you of scheduled activities, action point follow-ups, and the daily morning briefing.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger precision time-based notifications for your scheduled diary meetings, tours, and briefing alerts at your chosen time.
  • Device Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Ensures your scheduled activity and morning briefing reminders are restored if your phone is restarted.
  • Local Storage / Documents Access: Used exclusively when you explicitly choose to attach a PDF document, export a CSV report, or generate an encrypted backup file.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal or professional data with any third parties, advertisers, or government entities under any circumstances.

4. User Data Control and Deletion

You have 100% control over all data created in My Office Diary:

  • You can edit or delete individual entries, swipe to remove activities, or batch-delete records at any time within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android Settings permanently and irrevocably deletes all stored data from your device.
  • You can generate encrypted offline backups (.modbackup) which remain solely under your custody.

5. Children's Privacy

My Office Diary is an executive work tool intended for working professionals and students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
